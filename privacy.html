Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Plain-English privacy terms for Brenducate's SDK and cloud ledger.

1. Zero Access to Your LLM Provider Keys

The Brenducate Python SDK executes in-process inside your own application servers. Your OpenAI and Anthropic API keys stay in your environment and are never transmitted to Brenducate.

2. Metadata-Only Mode & Telemetry Content

When instrumenting runs, the SDK logs model identifiers, token breakdowns (uncached, cached reads, cache writes, reasoning, output), calculated USD spend, latency, and end-customer identifiers (tenant_id).

By default, truncated prompt previews (up to 500 characters) are captured to help debug agent steps. To enforce strict metadata-only telemetry where zero prompt or response text leaves your servers, initialize the SDK with Brenducate(capture_prompts=False) or set BRENDUCATE_CAPTURE_PROMPTS=false.

3. How We Store & Use Data

  • Telemetry and workspace accounts are stored in managed PostgreSQL (Neon) and served via Vercel serverless infrastructure.
  • No model training: Brenducate never trains foundation models or sells customer telemetry data.
  • Payment processing is handled exclusively by Stripe; we never store raw credit card numbers.

4. Deletion & Contact

To export or delete your workspace records, email brendan@brenducate.com.
